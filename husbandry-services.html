Overview

Husbandry Services with Hellas

Husbandry services cover the shore-side needs of a vessel and its crew while in port, from moving people to delivering spares and stores. Getting these right keeps the vessel operating and the crew supported.

Hellas Logistics & Shipping Agencies coordinates these arrangements with local service providers, so shipowners and ship managers have a dependable point of contact in port.

What our service includes

  • Crew change coordination
    Arrangements for crew joining and leaving the vessel.
  • Crew transportation and local transfers
    Transport between the vessel, airport, hotel or other points.
  • Delivery coordination for vessel spares and stores
    Receiving and delivering spares and stores to the vessel.
  • Launch boat arrangements, where required
    Launch boat support for transfers and deliveries where the port setup calls for it.
  • Medical assistance and other crew-related arrangements
    Help arranging medical attention and other crew needs ashore.
  • Fresh water and vessel supply coordination
    Coordination of fresh water and other supplies for the vessel.
  • Local logistics and general husbandry support
    General on-the-ground assistance as the vessel requires.
Good to know

What are husbandry services?

In shipping, husbandry means the shore-based services a vessel needs while in port that are not part of the formal agency paperwork: crew changes, transport, medical help, deliveries and supplies. We handle the coordination so the master and the office can focus on operations.

Who this service is for

  • Shipowners and ship managers
  • Operators needing reliable crew change support
  • Vessels requiring spares, stores or supplies in port

Details that help us respond faster

  • Vessel name and port
  • Expected arrival and departure dates
  • Number of crew joining or leaving and travel details
  • Description of spares, stores or supplies to deliver
  • Any medical or special requirements
  • Whether a launch boat is expected to be needed

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is included in husbandry services?

Crew change coordination, crew transport, delivery of spares and stores, launch boat arrangements where required, medical assistance, fresh water and supply coordination and general local support.

Can you coordinate a crew change?

Yes. We coordinate crew changes and local transfers. Please send the vessel details, dates and crew travel information.

Can you deliver spares to the vessel?

Yes. We coordinate delivery of vessel spares and stores to the vessel, including launch boat arrangements where required.

How quickly should we contact you?

As early as possible. Early notice gives us time to plan arrangements with local providers.
