Good to know

FCL or LCL: which is right for me?

FCL (Full Container Load) means one container is reserved for your cargo alone. It is usually the better choice for larger volumes, or when you want your goods kept separate from other shippers’ cargo.

LCL (Less than Container Load) means your cargo shares a container with other shipments. It lets you ship smaller quantities without paying for a whole container. Tell us your volume and timeline and we will help you compare.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL gives you the whole container for your cargo. LCL lets you share container space with other cargo, which suits smaller shipments.

What details do you need to give a freight rate?

Cargo description, weight and volume, loading and destination ports, container type if known, and your cargo ready date. The more detail you share, the more accurate our response.

Which documents are usually needed?

Requirements depend on the cargo and the countries involved. Typical documents include a commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading. We help coordinate the documentation for your shipment.

Do you ship worldwide?

We support worldwide ocean freight requirements through coordination with shipping lines and logistics partners. Services are subject to cargo requirements, trade routes and operational arrangements.
