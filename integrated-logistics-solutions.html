Integrated Logistics Solutions

Coordinated logistics services tailored to your operational requirements.

Overview

Integrated Logistics Solutions with Hellas

Some shipments need more than a single service. They need cargo movement planned end to end, documents kept in order and partners at both ends working together. Integrated logistics brings these pieces under one coordinated plan.

Hellas Logistics & Shipping Agencies works with you to understand your requirement, then coordinates overseas partners and local service providers so your cargo moves with clear communication throughout.

What our service includes

  • International shipping coordination
    Coordination of shipments moving across borders and between ports.
  • Import and export logistics support
    Support for the logistics steps involved in importing and exporting goods.
  • Cargo movement planning
    Planning the cargo’s movement to match your timeline and requirements.
  • Documentation assistance
    Help organising documentation for your shipment.
  • Coordination with overseas partners and local service providers
    One point of contact bringing together everyone involved.
Good to know

One partner, coordinated end to end

Instead of managing several providers separately, you can route your requirement through Hellas. We coordinate with shipping lines, overseas partners and local service providers so you have a single point of contact for planning, communication and follow-up.

Who this service is for

  • Businesses that want one coordinated partner for their shipments
  • Companies with import and export flows needing planning support
  • Partners needing local coordination in India

Details that help us respond faster

  • What you are shipping and where it is moving from and to
  • Volumes, timelines and any recurring schedule
  • Services you need (freight, handling, documentation, etc.)
  • Parties already involved at origin and destination
  • Any special requirements or constraints

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What does integrated logistics mean?

It means coordinating the different elements of a shipment, such as planning, freight, documentation and partner coordination, as one plan rather than separate tasks.

Do you work with overseas partners?

Yes. We coordinate with overseas partners and local service providers to support your cargo at origin and destination.

Can you help with documentation?

We offer documentation assistance as part of coordinating your shipment. Requirements vary by cargo and country.

How do I start?

Send us a short description of your requirement through the contact page or by email, and we will respond with the next steps.
