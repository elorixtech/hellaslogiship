About Hellas Logistics & Shipping Agencies

Reliable, efficient and cost-effective logistics and maritime services, built on clear communication and long-term partnerships.

Who we are

A Mangalore-based partner for global shipping

Hellas Logistics & Shipping Agencies is a Mangalore, India–headquartered logistics and shipping services company committed to delivering reliable, efficient and cost-effective solutions to the global maritime and logistics industry.

We specialise in worldwide ocean freight forwarding, ship agency and husbandry services, supporting shipowners, ship managers, freight partners, importers, exporters and businesses with their shipping and logistics requirements.

With a strong focus on operational efficiency, customer satisfaction and professional service, we aim to simplify complex shipping operations through dependable coordination and tailored logistics solutions.

At Hellas, we believe that every shipment and every vessel deserves dedicated attention, transparent communication and reliable support. Our goal is to build long-term business relationships by connecting global opportunities with seamless shipping solutions.

At a glance

  • Headquarters: Mangalore, Karnataka, India
  • Ocean freight forwarding – FCL & LCL
  • Ship agency & husbandry services
  • Serving shipowners, ship managers, importers & exporters
